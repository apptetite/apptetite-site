Timers that know
what comes next.

You can't cool a bake that hasn't been baked. MacroPlate reads each step, finds the timers, and locks the ones that have to wait.

  • ⏱️Tap to start. The timer turns amber and counts down.
  • 🔒Locked until ready. The next timer opens the moment this one finishes.
  • 🔔Time's up! It rings until you stop it, even with your phone locked.

This demo runs at 60× speed, so a minute takes one second.

Blueberry Protein Oat Bake

Serves 4 · 10 min prep · 30 min cook
312 cal P 24g C 38g F 7g
  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a 20 cm square dish.
  2. Mix the oats, protein powder, baking powder and cinnamon. Whisk in the eggs, milk, yoghurt and maple syrup.
  3. Fold in most of the blueberries, pour into the dish and scatter the rest on top.
  4. Bake for 28–32 minutes until set in the middle. Cool for 10 minutes and cut into 4.
